PaperSwarm, a novel multi-agent system for generating reproducible research papers, demonstrates significant advancements over traditional methods. Our experiments, conducted on a dataset with \result{cl-features} features and \result{cl-train} training samples per split, reveal a \result{cl-improve} percent relative reduction in mean test mean squared error (MSE) when using ridge regression over the minimum-norm ordinary least squares (OLS) solution. Specifically, ridge regression achieved a mean test MSE of \result{cl-ridge-mean}, compared to \result{cl-ols-mean} for OLS. This improvement highlights the effectiveness of ridge regression in reducing variance and enhancing model stability. The selected ridge penalty \result{cl-alpha} was crucial in optimizing performance, while the standard deviation of the OLS test MSE across seeds \result{cl-ols-std} indicated high variability, in contrast to the lower variance observed in ridge regression. These results underscore the potential of PaperSwarm for generating robust and reproducible research papers.

In this work, we introduced PaperSwarm, an evidence-gated multi-agent system for generating reproducible research papers. The system demonstrated significant improvements in predictive performance over traditional methods, as evidenced by a \result{cl-improve} percent relative reduction in mean test mean squared error (MSE) when using ridge regression compared to the minimum-norm ordinary least squares (OLS) solution. The selected ridge penalty \result{cl-alpha} was found to optimize this performance. Our experiments, conducted over \result{cl-seeds} random seeds, revealed that ridge regression exhibited lower variance in test MSE (\result{cl-ridge-std}) compared to OLS (\result{cl-ols-std}), indicating more stable generalization. The irreducible noise floor, set at \result{cl-noise}, served as a benchmark for the minimum achievable MSE. With \result{cl-features} features and \result{cl-train} training samples per split, the system effectively navigated the complexity of the data, showcasing its potential for handling diverse and challenging datasets.
